Getting Scholar's “Cited by” list without an API key
  1. Publish or Perish (free, harzing.com): run a Google Scholar search for the paper, look up its citing works, then save the results as CSV. Import that CSV here.
  2. Scholar “My library”: on the “Cited by” page, save papers with ☆, then in My library select them → Export → CSV or BibTeX.
  3. With a SerpApi key this page fetches Scholar's list itself. Choose “Google Scholar” as the source under the paper.
From your Zotero

Each imported paper is matched by DOI, or by title through Crossref and OpenAlex, to add the abstract, field, subfield and keywords. The match_method column shows how each paper was matched. Papers that can't be matched keep the data from your file.

Which paper did you mean?

Tick every version of the paper you want: databases often list the arXiv preprint and the published article separately, and each has its own citations. Ticking both combines them without duplicates.
